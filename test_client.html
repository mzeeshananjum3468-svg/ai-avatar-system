Video states: idle — greeting loop shown before the first message and after a response fully finishes. thinking — loop shown once a request is in flight but no chunk is playing yet (right after Send, and in the gap between two chunks if a chunk finishes downloading before the next one is ready). chunk:N — an actual response chunk playing. The tag in the top-right of the stage shows which one is active.
Barge-in behaviour: hitting Send (or Interrupt) while the avatar is speaking immediately clears the pending chunk queue, snaps the player back to the idle loop, bumps the internal generation counter, and sends the new message on the same open socket — matching the run_barge_in scenario in your test script. Chunks belonging to the interrupted response that are still downloading get discarded on arrival instead of being enqueued.
